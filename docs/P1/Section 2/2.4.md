# 2.4 Constraints

1. **Repository constraint**

   The project team must use an instructor-approved repository for all project work.

   **Source:** Course project requirements.

2. **Project evidence constraint**

   The team must maintain verifiable repository evidence of issues, changes, reviews, tests, and individual contributions throughout the project.

   **Source:** Course project requirements.

3. **Schedule constraint**

   The project must be developed and submitted according to the three course milestones: P1 on October 18, P2 on November 8, and P3 on December 9.

   **Source:** Course project deliverable schedule.

4. **Team constraint**

   The project must be completed collaboratively by the assigned course team, with individual contributions remaining identifiable and verifiable.

   **Source:** Course syllabus.

5. **User eligibility constraint**

   Access to the Carpool System shall be limited to verified members of the university community.

   **Source:** Team product decision.
