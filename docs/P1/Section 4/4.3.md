# R1:
not enough students participate. Making the demand for rides insufficient and impacting number of rides. 

Liklihood: 
Medium 

Impact:
High 

Mitigation--Action and Owner: 
Identify the minimum features needed to make riding as straightfoward and easy as possible. Review this idea with commuter students and gather feedback before P2. 

# R2 
difficulty implemeting key features, delaying development. 

Liklihood: 
Medium

Impact: 
High

Mitigation--Action and Owner: 
Identify the features that will be more complicated and will require the most work, first. Possibly complete a feasibility test befroe development of those features. Overall have a structured plan and schedule for each key feature so we know what feature may take more time and plan accordingly. 

# R3
Students may not provide enough infromation about their pickup location. Likely those who live in apartments and such. This impacts ride coordination and may lead to the cancellation of some rides or users being late. 

Liklihood: 
Medium 

Impact: 
Medium 

Mitigation--Action and Owner: 
Define location reauirements for ride information an also document how students can update or cancel incorrect ride/location details. 

# R4

Liklihood: Medium 

Impact: High 

Mitigation-- Action and Owner: 
Define access-control and privacy reqiurements before P2
Also review which information drivers and riders can access.
