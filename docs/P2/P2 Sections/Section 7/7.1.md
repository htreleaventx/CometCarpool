# 7.1 Architecture Decision Records

## Overview

This section documents the major architectural decisions made during the design of the Comet Carpool System.

Each Architecture Decision Record (ADR) explains the context of a decision, the alternatives considered, the selected approach, and the consequences of that choice.

At least three ADRs are required. ADR-01 documents the architectural style selected in Section 6.2.
