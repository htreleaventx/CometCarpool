# Q19 - Suggested Contribution Amounts

## Question
Can drivers suggest a contribution amount when posting a ride?

## Final Answer
No, drivers will suggest contribution amounts in person or through in-app messaging.

## Reasoning
Because contributions are voluntary, the app does not need to facilitate them. Implementing a suggested amount may adversely pressure riders to contribute, much like a restaurant's suggested tip system.

## Impact
- Requirements: FR-12 should be limited to a simple agreement without any specific amounts being suggested.
- Use Cases: UC-05 should incorporate cost=sharing agreements without suggested contribution amounts.
- Design/Architecture: N/A
