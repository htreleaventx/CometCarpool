### Observations for the Team

1. **S3 and the moderation approach (Q6).** S3's need is to "review reported activity" and "manage user accounts". Q6 decided the team will not actively moderate or resolve disputes, and will use automatic suspension and banning instead. The notes in `UseCaseAnalysis.md` also point out that P1 does not describe how an administrator reviews reports. FR-14 and Section 2.3 still list an administrator. The team should decide whether S3 remains a stakeholder with a limited role, such as managing the automatic suspension rules and system data, or whether S3's needs should be reworded.
2. **Evidence for S1 to S4.** The needs for the four external stakeholders rest on assumptions. This is acceptable for P1 but is the weakest part of this section for P2.
