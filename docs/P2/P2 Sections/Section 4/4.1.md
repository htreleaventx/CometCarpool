## Selection Justification

Explain why these use cases were selected for detailed analysis.

UC-03 - Search and Manage Ride Requests was selected because searching for rides and posting ride requests are both essential features for this platofrm. this use case supports FR-04, FR-05, FR-06, and FR-11. It also involves important quality reqiurements such as searchperformance and reliable data storage. Potential altenrative flows include no matching rides being found, missing trip information, and request cancellations. 

UC-04 - Manage Recurring Schedules and Find Matches was selected becasue recurring transportation is an important part of the carpool system we are trying to develop. This use case supports FR-07, FR-08, FR-09 by allowing students to record their recurring transportation needs or availability and identify compatible schedules. It involves more complex system behavior because rider and driver schedules must be compared. Potential issues include missing schedule information, no compatible matches, and changes to previously saved schedules. 

UC_02 - Post and Manage Rider Offers was chosen because drviers must be able to create ride offers for students to find available transportation. This use case supports FR-03, FR-05, FR-11 and FR-15. It involves validating trip information, managing available passenger seats, and saving ride postings. Potential alternate flows include incomplete ride information, invalid seat counts, ride cancellations, and updates to existing postings. 

All together, these use cases cover the main process of drivers offering up rides, riders searching for transportation, and students finding compatible recurring schdules. They were also selected because they contain multiple actor-system interactions, possible failures, and alternate flows that require detailed analysis. 

Consider the following:

- Importance to the overall Comet Carpool System.
- Relationship to functional and quality requirements.
- Complexity of interactions between actors and the system.
- Potential failures or alternate flows.
- Risks or uncertainties that need further analysis.
